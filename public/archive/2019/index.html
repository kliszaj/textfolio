About Me

I’m a senior UX designer and researcher, currently working on experiences that empower people to use technology more thoughtfully at North.

Over the last 7 years, I've designed experiences and systems for multiple software and hardware platforms. I'm able to ideate from first principles, quickly iterate on my concepts, prototype them, and evaluate them through a structured mixed-methods research approach. I’m especially interested in humane technology, wearable computing, and designing interactions for physical interfaces.

When I’m not thinking about design, you can find me on the trails (running or biking), endurance racing, rock climbing, playing guitar in a polka-punk band, and tinkering with technology.
